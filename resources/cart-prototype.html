Tilted — Batch Turn / “Shopping Cart” Prototype

A turn = act on every hand where it’s on you, off one shared chip stack. This prototype lets you feel three ways to structure that, plus how strict the chip budget should be. Switch modes on the left — the phone re-renders live. The scenario: Round 3, you’re SB, 8 hands await you, 900 chips available.

Try this (the reallocation you described)

  1. Open Hand 3 → Raise, drag the slider up big (~600).
  2. Watch Available drop and In cart rise.
  3. Open Hand 7 → try All-in. Notice there aren’t enough chips.
  4. Go back to Hand 3, change it to Check — chips return.
  5. Re-open Hand 7 → All-in now funds fully. Then Submit.
