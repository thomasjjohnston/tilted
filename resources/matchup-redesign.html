Heads-Up Match Redesign

The Match-Up surface gets a poker-table metaphor: a focused, dealer-buttoned felt for the hand on action, with a 10-hand strip beneath that keeps your whole match in sight. New full-screen "Hand Won" moment makes opponent folds satisfying. Turn report is rebuilt with explicit street context, real per-hand math, and four layout variants to compare. Waiting-for-opponent gets three drill-down options.

Design notes for review

  • Dealer button position — In a heads-up round, you're either the SB or BB for the entire round (all 10 hands). The button stays on the same side for the whole round; only the cards/board differ. So the button on every mini-puck reinforces the same fact ten times — solving the "wait, am I SB or BB here?" issue.
  • Per-hand 0 bug — Server should persist a resolved_amount snapshot on hand close (the +/− at the moment of resolution), not derive net from myReserved after settlement (which is zeroed when chips move into total_chips). All four turn-report variants source from that snapshot, so per-hand math always matches the bottom-line net.
  • Fold context — Every fold carries its street ("folded preflop" / "folded turn" / "folded river"). Preflop folds are styled as "stole blinds" (low-stakes, calmer); river folds are styled as the big payday. The full-screen Hand Won moment scales the celebration to the pot size.
  • Waiting visibility — All three waiting variants share the same puck-strip vocabulary as the main turn view (gold dot = action-on-opponent, hourglass = mid-hand waiting on board, ✓/✗ = resolved), so users can scan the strip and immediately know match state.
  • Keep the heads-up animation — The match-start animation isn't shown here; this redesign sits underneath it. The new felt-table look should feel like a natural continuation of that animation rather than a different surface.
