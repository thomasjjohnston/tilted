The flow

  1. Start on the board, Hand 1. See board cards, pot, opp action, your cards.
  2. Act, then Next › through all 8 hands.
  3. Last hand → Review turn lands on the cart.
  4. Tap any cart row → back to that hand’s board to change it.
  5. Submit turn sends all 8 at once.
